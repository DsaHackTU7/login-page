About Recyclo

Turning industrial waste into valuable resources through smart connections.

Our Story

Recyclo was built on a simple but powerful idea — waste for one industry can be raw material for another. Every day, factories and production units discard tons of usable materials simply because the right buyer never finds them.

We saw an opportunity to change that. Instead of letting valuable resources end up in landfills, Recyclo creates a digital bridge between industries. Sellers can list surplus or by-product materials, and buyers can discover affordable, reusable alternatives for their own operations.

What started as a sustainability-driven concept quickly evolved into a full marketplace focused on efficiency, transparency, and environmental impact. Our platform helps businesses reduce disposal costs, source materials at lower prices, and move toward more responsible production practices.

At Recyclo, we believe industrial growth and environmental responsibility can go hand in hand. By turning waste into opportunity, we’re helping build a circular economy where resources stay in use for longer and industries grow smarter, not wasteful.
